First Aid Quick Guides

Choking

  1. Ask "Are you choking?" — if they can cough or speak, encourage coughing.
  2. If they can't breathe/speak, give 5 back blows between the shoulder blades.
  3. Follow with 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver).
  4. Repeat until the object is expelled or help arrives. Call emergency services if it doesn't clear quickly.

Burns

  1. Cool the burn under cool (not ice-cold) running water for 10-20 minutes.
  2. Remove nearby jewelry/clothing before swelling starts.
  3. Cover loosely with a clean, non-fluffy cloth or cling film.
  4. Don't apply ice, butter, or ointments. Seek care for large, deep, or facial burns.

Cuts & Bleeding

  1. Apply firm, direct pressure with a clean cloth.
  2. Raise the injured area above heart level if possible.
  3. Once bleeding slows, clean gently and cover with a sterile dressing.
  4. Seek care if bleeding won't stop, is spurting, or the wound is deep/gaping.

CPR (adult, general steps)

  1. Check responsiveness and breathing. Call emergency services immediately.
  2. Place hands centered on the chest, push hard and fast (about 100-120/min).
  3. Allow full chest recoil between compressions.
  4. Continue until help arrives or the person responds. Formal certified training is strongly recommended.

About Vitals

Vitals is a custom-built health information interface. Questions are sent to a large language model through an API, using a system prompt designed to keep answers general, safety-conscious, and free of diagnoses or prescription dosing.

Tools like the symptom checker, BMI calculator, and trackers run entirely in your browser — no data leaves your device except the text/images you choose to send in chat, and nothing is stored after you close the tab.

Vitals does not replace professional medical care.
